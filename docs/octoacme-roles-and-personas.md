# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support delivery health by aligning technical execution, team capacity, and cross-functional priorities. They translate strategy into workable delivery plans while protecting engineering quality and focus.

### Responsibilities
- Balance team capacity, staffing, and priorities across workstreams
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on sequencing and milestones
- Support technical planning, dependency management, and execution trade-offs
- Coach engineers and remove delivery blockers
- Help maintain a sustainable engineering rhythm and healthy team practices

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Reduce bottlenecks and unplanned work
- Maintain a high-performing engineering organization

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering delivery reviews
- Planning and staffing discussions with PMs and Product Leads
- Escalation conversations for delivery risk or resourcing constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to guide technical execution and improve quality of delivery
- Aligns with Project Managers on timelines, dependencies, and risk communication
- Partners with Product Managers on scope trade-offs and sequencing decisions

---

## Security and Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure the project follows required controls, secure engineering practices, and regulatory obligations. They help teams identify and mitigate risk before it becomes a production issue.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements, architecture decisions, and risky dependencies
- Define or validate control requirements for data handling, access, and compliance
- Coordinate security assessments, threat reviews, and remediation planning
- Support incident readiness and escalation paths for security events
- Help balance security requirements with delivery speed and business goals

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the lifecycle
- Build secure-by-default practices into delivery work
- Improve confidence in release readiness and operational safety

### Typical Communication
- Design review checkpoints and security risk reviews
- Release and deployment readiness checks
- Security escalation updates and incident coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on secure implementation patterns and remediation planning
- Works with Project Managers to ensure risk and mitigation actions are tracked
- Supports Product Managers by clarifying security trade-offs and compliance constraints

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, validation strategy, and release readiness criteria. They ensure that features are testable, defects are visible early, and the team can confidently ship increments.

### Responsibilities
- Define the quality strategy, test coverage goals, and validation approach
- Partner with engineering and product teams on acceptance criteria and release gates
- Review test plans, automation opportunities, and manual QA needs
- Identify quality risks early in the lifecycle and recommend mitigations
- Support release readiness and post-deployment validation

### Goals
- Reduce defects escaping into production
- Improve confidence in the quality of each increment
- Align testing practices with product and delivery needs

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and acceptance review sessions
- Release readiness sign-offs and defect triage
- Quality status updates in sprint and milestone reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers on testability, automation, and bug validation
- Provides Product Managers with evidence on readiness and quality gaps
- Coordinates with Project Managers to ensure QA milestones and release gates are tracked

---

## Stakeholder Advocates / Customer Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Advocates represent business, customer, or partner interests throughout the project lifecycle. They keep user needs, stakeholder expectations, and decision context visible to the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Represent stakeholder priorities and desired outcomes
- Clarify business context, constraints, and trade-offs
- Help validate that delivered work meets real user and business needs
- Surface risks related to adoption, communication, and stakeholder alignment
- Support milestone reviews and decision-making with clear feedback

### Goals
- Keep customer value central to delivery decisions
- Improve alignment between stakeholders and the team
- Increase confidence that outcomes match expectations

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder check-ins and milestone reviews
- Feedback loops during discovery, demos, and release readiness
- Decision and prioritization discussions with PMs and Product Leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Brings customer and business context to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Helps Developers understand the “why” behind requirements and priorities
- Provides validation input for acceptance criteria and release decisions

---

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams use evidence to measure value, monitor outcomes, and improve decisions. They connect product and operational metrics to project progress and business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define dashboards, KPIs, and success metrics for the project
- Track release impact, adoption, and project health indicators
- Help interpret performance data and identify trends or risks
- Support decision-making with evidence from usage, quality, and operational metrics
- Partner with stakeholders to align reporting to business goals

### Goals
- Improve decision quality with measurable evidence
- Detect issues early through clear monitoring and reporting
- Show the connection between delivery work and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metric reviews and milestone reporting
- Insights shared during stakeholder updates and retrospectives
- Dashboards and status summaries for Product and Project leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers measure whether outcomes are being achieved
- Supports Project Managers with progress tracking and risk visibility
- Provides Developers and QA teams with telemetry to improve feature quality and reliability

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The complete set of persona definitions helps teams clarify ownership, accountability, and decision-making across planning, execution, release, and operations.
