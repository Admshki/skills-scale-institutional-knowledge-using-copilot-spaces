# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection provides a shared, lightweight guide to how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and improves cross-functional projects. OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative, and data-informed approach with clear ownership, regular communication, and a focus on measurable outcomes.

## Project management process

The project lifecycle connects the documents in this collection:

1. **Initiation** — Validate the problem and desired outcome, identify stakeholders, define success measures, establish an initial timeline and risks, and approve the work for planning.
2. **Planning** — Break approved work into shippable increments, prioritize and estimate the backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, and map milestones, risks, dependencies, and responsibilities.
3. **Execution and tracking** — Manage delivery through a project board, use small reviewed pull requests, run CI and quality checks, track progress and success metrics, and escalate blockers through defined levels.
4. **Risk management and communication** — Maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, and provide regular stakeholder updates through a single source of truth, with escalation paths for issues that need broader attention.
5. **Release and deployment** — Verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, rollback planning, and post-deployment communications before and after production release.
6. **Retrospective and continuous improvement** — After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture what went well, identify improvements, assign action items with owners and due dates, and measure their impact.

The core project roles are Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and stakeholders. The roles and personas document also describes how developer, product manager, and project manager personas are used in exercises and role-specific guidance.
